Material Forge 0.1.0 (test build)
=================================

A PBR 3D texture painter in the spirit of Substance 3D Painter.

Getting started
- Start Material Forge, then File > New project (or drop an .fbx / .obj /
  .gltf / .glb on the window). Each material of the mesh becomes a texture set.
- Bake mesh maps (left tool strip) for the generators and smart materials.
- Drag materials and smart materials from Assets onto the layer stack or
  the mesh. Paint with 1 (brush), 2 (eraser), 4 (polygon fill), 6 (clone,
  V sets the source).
- Camera: Alt + left drag orbits, Alt + middle drag pans, Alt + right drag
  or the wheel zooms. F frames the mesh.
- File > Export textures writes the maps (presets for common engines).

Requirements
- Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit, with a DirectX 12 or Vulkan capable GPU.

Where things are kept
- Your library, layout and autosaves: %APPDATA%\SP_RETRO
