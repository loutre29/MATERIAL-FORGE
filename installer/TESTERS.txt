Material Forge 0.3.0 (test build)
=================================

A PBR 3D texture painter in the spirit of Substance 3D Painter.

Getting started
- Start Material Forge, then open the menu at the top left > File > New
  project (or drop an .fbx / .obj / .gltf / .glb on the window). Each
  material of the mesh becomes a texture set.
- Top right: Auto UV (new UVs, for meshes without good ones), Bake (mesh
  maps for the generators and smart materials), Export (the textures,
  with presets for common engines).
- Drag materials and smart materials from the Library (right) onto the
  layer stack (left) or onto the mesh.
- Tools (Photoshop style keys): B brush, Shift + B projection, E eraser,
  S clone, R smudge, P path, G paint bucket / gradient, U polygon fill,
  M / L / W / Q selections, I color picker. [ and ] change the brush
  size, Ctrl + drag changes size, hardness, flow and angle.
- Camera: Alt + left drag orbits, Alt + middle drag pans, Alt + right drag
  or the wheel zooms. F frames the mesh. F1 / F2 / F3: 3D + 2D, 3D only,
  2D only. In the 2D view, Alt + left drag turns the view.
- Typed numbers can go past the end of a slider (click the value box).

Requirements
- Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit, with a DirectX 12 or Vulkan capable GPU.

Where things are kept
- Your library, layout, shortcuts and autosaves: %APPDATA%\SP_RETRO

Please report: what you did, what you expected, what happened (a screenshot
helps). Edit > Preferences (Ctrl + K) lists every shortcut and lets you
change them.
